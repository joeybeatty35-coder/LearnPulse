LearnPulse Community Rules & Guardrails

LearnPulse is building a community-driven space where people can exchange practical knowledge: DIY, learning methods, arts & crafts, and life systems. To keep it useful and respectful, we run clear guardrails and do not allow content that turns into arguments or harm.

What this community is for

DIY + home projects learning methods + study systems arts & crafts + creative builds calm peer support + problem-solving
  • Share what worked, what failed, and what you learned.
  • Ask for help respectfully. Give help with clarity and kindness.
  • Encourage small daily wins: progress on bad days still counts.
  • Keep it practical: steps, materials, photos, and safety notes.

Anonymous option: you can use a nickname and a small profile image. Don’t post private personal data. If you share photos, remove sensitive details first.

Hard boundaries (non-negotiable)

No politics or religion debates No harassment, hate, or threats No sexual content No spam, scams, or affiliate drops

We keep this space focused on learning and useful action. If a topic tends to become a “tug of war,” it doesn’t belong here. Repeat violations can lead to removal and bans.

We will never: ask you for passwords, private documents, bank info, or “favors.” Any message claiming to be LearnPulse requesting money outside official checkout links is not us.

Report an issue Support the free content

Posting rules (simple + enforceable)

1) Be respectful

No insults, mockery, or “dunking.” Critique ideas, not people. Keep language clean.

2) No personal data

Don’t post addresses, phone numbers, IDs, private emails, or doxxing info—yours or anyone else’s.

3) Safety notes required

If a DIY involves tools, heat, electricity, chemicals, or ladders: include clear safety steps.

4) No medical/mental-health treatment claims

Share routines and support, but no “this cures” claims. Encourage professional help when needed.

5) No spam or scams

No unsolicited promos, link dumping, referral bait, or suspicious downloads.

6) Keep it on-topic

DIY, learning, crafts, and life systems. Off-topic arguments will be removed.

Emotional intelligence rule: if you feel heated, pause. Ask one honest question. The goal is “brain and heart on the same page”—clear action + calm intention.

Compliance notes (Google Play friendly)

  • This page is informational and sets behavior expectations.
  • No political or religious content categories are hosted or promoted.
  • No sexual content is permitted.
  • No direct requests for personal data. No newsletters or subscription harvesting.
  • Users are encouraged to report abuse and safety issues via Support.

Informational only: results vary. Nothing here is medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or tax advice.
